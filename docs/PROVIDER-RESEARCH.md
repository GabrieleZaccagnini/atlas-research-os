The PlanB composite's live legend lists price coloured by RSI, realized price, 200WMA and a 2024-refit stock-to-flow model; [its framework](https://docs.blockhorizon.io/chart-tutorials/planb/planb-market-cycle) treats these as cycle context. They are candidates, not yet Atlas embeds. Individual profit/supply signals overlap the Cycle Index and should be added only if their underlying detail is needed.

## Bitbo cycle charts and independent Atlas views (2026-10-04)

The user selected eleven [Bitbo chart references](https://charts.bitbo.io/) for Market Structure. [Bitbo pricing](https://charts.bitbo.io/pricing/) and its [API information](https://charts.bitbo.io/btc-api/) place API access on a paid tier and do not establish permission to embed or reproduce its chart data in Atlas. D-033 therefore uses independent Blockchain.com daily BTC price, network hash-rate and gross miner-revenue histories for Atlas-labeled calculations. The realized-price selection points to the existing BlockHorizon-hosted chart. The fitted rainbow/power-law bands and halving-relative range intentionally have Atlas's own stated methods; they are not copies of Bitbo's model parameters. Reference links remain visible for comparison.

Two requested series are still unavailable in Atlas: short-term holder realized price requires age-cohort cost-basis data, and ETF flows versus newly issued BTC requires a dated, consistently defined ETF holdings/flow feed. [ResearchBitcoin's short-term holder metric](https://researchbitcoin.net/metrics/realized_price_sth/) advertises an access tier but would require account/token and response validation. [Newhedge's ETF-flow API](https://prod-docs.newhedge.io/api/spot-bitcoin-etf-total-net-flows) documents an endpoint but also requires access. Neither has been connected or validated live. Do not derive these two series from spot price or substitute ETF price performance for actual flows.

# Atlas Vision

Atlas is a crypto intelligence and research operating system designed to help identify high-conviction opportunities before they become mainstream.

## Core Mission

Map the market. Find the signal. Build conviction.

## Core Philosophy

- Markets are probabilistic, not predictable.
- Research builds conviction.
- Price does not always equal value.
- Macro provides context.
- Market cycles provide timing context.
- Narratives drive capital rotation.
- Fundamentals determine long-term value.
- Technical analysis helps with timing and risk management.
- Every thesis must include an invalidation thesis.
- The system should challenge confirmation bias, not reinforce it.

## Primary Questions Atlas Should Answer

1. Where are we in the market cycle?
2. Where is liquidity moving?
3. What narratives are emerging?
4. Which projects appear fundamentally undervalued?
5. What could invalidate the investment thesis?
6. What deserves attention today?
